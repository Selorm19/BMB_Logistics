Custom School Apparel & Branded Accessories

Uniform fabrics, custom T-shirts, sports kits, socks, badges, caps, and school bags designed to reflect your brand identity.

At B-Squared Logistics, we understand that a school's uniform is more than just clothing—it is a symbol of pride, equality, and institutional identity. We deliver high-durability fabrics and precision tailoring built to endure daily wear while keeping students comfortable and smart.

Our Apparel & Accessory Offerings

  • Uniform Fabrics & Tailoring: High-grade cotton-poly blends, check fabrics, pinafores, trousers, skirts, and tailored blazers crafted for durability and color retention.
  • Sports & House Kits: Breathable, moisture-wicking jerseys, shorts, tracksuits, and house-system T-shirts for physical education and athletic events.
  • Embroidered Badges & Crests: Precision machine-embroidered crests, woven patches, metal pins, and custom school ties.
  • Branded Accessories: Custom-printed backpacks, duffel bags, socks, neckties, belts, and sun caps styled with school colors and logos.

"Consistent, high-quality apparel instills a sense of belonging in students while projecting prestige and excellence to the wider community."

Quality Standards & Bulk Logistics

We source shrink-resistant, colorfast materials that hold up against continuous washing and active playground use. Whether you need an intake batch for a new academic session or ongoing replenishment stock, our bulk production pipeline guarantees timely delivery ahead of term dates.
