Digitize Your Educational Environment with School Connect

Deployment and staff training for comprehensive E-School management software to digitize your educational environment, powered by School Connect.

Transitioning to a fully digital school management ecosystem reduces administrative overhead, minimizes paperwork errors, and enhances parent-teacher collaboration. BMB Logistics provides full implementation, cloud integration, and hands-on staff onboarding for our signature software platform, School Connect.

Key Features of School Connect

  • Student Information & Academic Records: Centralized databases for student profiles, enrollment tracking, grade book management, and automated report card generation.
  • Fee Management & Digital Payments: Secure online fee collection portals, automated receipting, payment reminders, and real-time financial tracking.
  • Attendance & Parent Communication: Instant SMS/email notifications for student attendance, performance updates, and school announcements via the parent portal.
  • Staff & Payroll Management: Simplified teacher scheduling, performance evaluations, attendance logs, and administrative HR tools.

"School Connect simplifies campus operations into a single, intuitive platform, enabling educators to focus on teaching rather than administration."

Deployment & Onboarding Pipeline

Software is only as good as its implementation. Our dedicated technical team handles full system configuration, data migration from legacy formats, and interactive training workshops for administrative staff, teachers, and school leadership to ensure a seamless transition.
