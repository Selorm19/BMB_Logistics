Customized Educational Print & Publishing Services

BMB Logistics produces high-grade printed learning materials tailored specifically for institutions, schools, and educational boards. We handle end-to-end production—from design layout and press runs to regional distribution.

What We Deliver

  • Custom Branded Exercise Books: Custom laminated covers featuring school crests, mission statements, and custom page rulings (graph, broad lines, unruled).
  • Textbook Publishing & Distribution: High-volume press runs for core curriculum textbooks, workbooks, and teacher manuals.
  • Institutional Stationery: Custom school diaries, report cards, certificates, answer sheets, and administrative registers.
  • Examination Material Security: Sealed, high-integrity printing and timed distribution of exam papers and answer booklets.

"Our print solutions help institutions build strong visual identities while delivering durable, high-quality study tools to students."

Why Choose Our Publishing Division?

We utilize industrial offset and high-speed digital printing processes, ensuring rich ink saturation, heavy paper stocks (70gsm to 300gsm covers), and durable saddle-stitch or perfect binding built to withstand daily classroom use.
