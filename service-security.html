Comprehensive Campus Security & Access Control Infrastructure

Expert installation of CCTV surveillance networks and biometric time management systems for campus safety.

Ensuring the physical safety of students, faculty, and institutional assets requires reliable, real-time security systems. B-Squared Logistics designs and deploys robust IP surveillance networks and access control frameworks customized for educational campuses, administrative facilities, and commercial buildings.

Core Security & Management Systems

  • High-Definition CCTV Networks: Installation of indoor/outdoor IP cameras, thermal imaging, perimeter coverage, and NVR recording systems with remote monitoring capabilities.
  • Biometric Attendance & Access Control: Facial recognition and fingerprint terminals for staff timekeeping, automated gates, and secure door locks for restricted areas.
  • Perimeter Intrusion Detection: Electric fencing, motion sensors, and automated gate barriers to safeguard school boundaries against unauthorized access.
  • Centralized Security Control Hubs: Multi-screen monitor setups and central server configurations for continuous security team oversight.

"Protecting your community and institutional property starts with visible, automated, and dependable security infrastructure."

Turnkey Installation & Maintenance

Our team manages every phase of deployment—including cabling, camera positioning, software integration, and staff training. We also offer preventive maintenance packages to keep your cameras recording and access control systems functioning 24/7 without fail.
