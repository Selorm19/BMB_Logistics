Contact Our Team
How do I order custom branded school supplies?
Simply reach out via our contact page with your school crest and requirements. We handle customization for uniforms, badges, ties, and bags.
Is there a minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQs vary depending on the product line. Custom printing (like exercise books) and bespoke apparel typically have minimum runs. Contact us for specific details.
Do you handle nationwide deliveries?
Yes. We have a robust logistics network capable of distributing supplies safely and on time to institutions across the country.
Do you provide installation for IT and security systems?
Absolutely. Our technical team handles full end-to-end installation for CCTV setups, time management systems, and playground structures, including staff training.
